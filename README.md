# DOOM 67
Welcome to DOOM 67, a thrilling experience where your mission is clear: **bring back order**.  
Achieve your mission, no matter what. Those who dare to block you from achieving your goal shall be banished from life.

It all started with some rebels who claimed to be worthy of heaven. They pretended that hell was too harsh of a solution for their sins.  
What once ruled by clear leadership and boundaries quickly became a land of chaos and protestation. That's why the Goddess of Hell, with the permission of the almighty ruler of the world, ra9, decided to send 4 brave souls at hell's 4 corners to bring back order, justice and supress every rebels you may find on your way to accomplish your mission.

## Usage
By default, the game loads a default save file (plr.sav). However, you can totally load a custom save file by providing its name:
``./wolf3d [map_name.sav]``

## Keys
The game supports both **keyboard + mouse** & **controller** controls.

### KnM
- _zqsd_: to move (forward, left, back, right)
- _left / right arrows_: rotate the camera
- _o / p_: change weapons (left / right)
- _left shift_: run
- _left click_: fire

### Controller
(taking a dualshock 4 as example)
- Joystick left
- Joystick right
- l1 / r1
- //
- r2
