The Story

It all started with some rebels who claimed to be worthy of heaven. They pretended that hell was too harsh of a solution for their sins. What once ruled by clear leadership and boundaries quickly became a land of chaos and protestation.

That's why the Goddess of Hell, with the permission of the almighty ruler of the world, ra9, decided to send 4 brave souls at hell's 4 corners to bring back order, justice, and suppress every rebel you may find on your way.

Your mission is clear: bring back order. Achieve your mission, no matter what. Those who dare to block you from achieving your goal shall be banished from life.

Features

Unlimited FPS

No framerate cap. While AAA games ship locked at 60 FPS for full price, DOOM 67 runs as fast as your hardware allows. Pure, unthrottled performance.

Fluid Movement

Wall-sliding, extremely low input latency, and full controller support. Movement that feels immediate and responsive — the way it should be.

Colossal Scale

Maps up to 255×255 tiles — that's up to 65,025 traversable cells. Dungeons, labyrinths, spirals, arenas and mazes await.

Data Security

Advanced checksum system protects against data corruption and save tampering. Storage optimized to be 3.8× lighter than raw format.

Load Your Own Maps

Total freedom — load custom maps with a documented save format. The save system is designed for easy extensibility.

Keyboard & Controller

Full keyboard & mouse support with ZQSD layout, plus native DualShock 4 controller support. Play your way.
